Privacy notice

What the chat assistant collects

If you use the chat assistant, it asks for your email address and mobile phone number (your name is optional), and only after you tick the consent box. If you send a course enquiry, a support request or a message to the teacher, we also store what you write and the details you choose in that form, the page you were on, and the time.

Why

Only so the teacher can reply to you and manage your enquiry. We do not sell your details and we do not ask for passwords or payment-card details in the chat.

Who can see it

Only the teacher and administrators of this website. Other visitors can never see your details or your conversation.

Deleting your data

You can ask for your chat details to be deleted at any time: open the chat assistant, choose Contact Support, and ask for your data to be deleted. We will delete it and confirm.

Your browser

The chat remembers your details and language in your own browser so you do not have to retype them. Choosing "Not you?" in the chat clears them from the browser.
